How to create a Gmail App Password?
Never use your normal Google account password. Google requires a 16-character App Password generated with 2-Step Verification.

Security Principles

  • 100% Localhost Execution: Credentials never leave your machine and are excluded from Git via .gitignore.
  • No Passwords in SQLite: The SQLite database tracks non-sensitive history only; credentials reside in local .env.
  • Zero Paid/Third-Party APIs: Direct standard TLS communication with your email provider.
Gmail App Password Checklist:
  1. Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled on your Google Account.
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Name the app "SmartApply" and generate the 16-character code.
  4. Paste the code into the App Password field above.
