Help & Setup Guide

Instructions for configuring Gmail App Passwords, troubleshooting SMTP connections, and safe outreach practices.

How to Generate a Gmail App Password

Google Security Requirement
Important: Google disables direct login with your personal Gmail password for external SMTP clients. You must generate a dedicated 16-character App Password. This requires 2-Step Verification to be enabled on your account.
1
Enable 2-Step Verification

Visit your Google Account Security page. Under the "How you sign in to Google" section, ensure 2-Step Verification is turned ON.

2
Open Google App Passwords

Navigate directly to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. If prompted, verify your password.

3
Generate App Password

Enter an app name such as SmartApply in the App name field and click Create. Google will display a 16-character code (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop).

4
Paste into SmartApply

Copy the 16 characters (spaces are optional) and paste them into the App Password field in SMTP Settings. Click Test Connection to confirm verification.

Responsible Outreach

Individual Messaging

SmartApply never puts recipients in CC or BCC fields. Every recruiter receives an isolated, individual email with their customized details.

Configured Delays

Keep a delay of at least 2.0 to 5.0 seconds between emails. Email providers (like Gmail) will throttle or flag accounts that blast dozens of emails per second.

Small Batch Limits

We recommend sending in small batches of 5 to 10 applications per session. This ensures high deliverability, allows you to review responses, and protects your sender reputation.
